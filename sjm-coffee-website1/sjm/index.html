Who we are

SJM Coffee is a neighborhood café and bakery. We believe a good cup and a warm loaf make any day better, so we roast our beans in small batches and bake our breads before the doors open.

Our mission is to serve honest coffee at fair prices, and to give students, workers and families a place to slow down.
